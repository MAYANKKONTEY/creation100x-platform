# Supplier Outreach Templates

**Status: DRAFTS — do not send without founder approval.** Replace `[brackets]`. Use your own business name and real contact details only.

## 1. CJdropshipping agent / product sourcing request
> Subject: Sourcing request — silicone dog lick mat bundle, US warehouse
>
> Hello [agent name],
>
> I'm [your name] from [brand], an online store based in India selling to customers in the United States and Australia. I'm evaluating CJ as our fulfilment partner for a first product and would appreciate details on the following:
>
> **Product:** food-grade silicone dog lick mat, approx. 20 × 20 cm, with suction cups, in two groove patterns, plus a small silicone spatula. Reference CJ SKU (if found): [SKU].
>
> 1. Unit price at 1–50 / 51–200 / 200+ orders per month.
> 2. Is this SKU stocked in your US warehouse? Which variants, and current quantity?
> 3. Is import duty already paid on US-warehouse stock, or charged per order?
> 4. Shipping cost and delivery time from the US warehouse to ZIP 10001 and 90001; and China to Sydney (2000) via CJPacket.
> 5. Material test reports available: FDA 21 CFR 177.2600 and/or LFGB? Please share PDF copies.
> 6. Can you bundle 2 mats + 1 spatula in one unbranded polybag, and add a printed insert card I supply? Cost?
> 7. Defect policy: how are damaged / defective units handled — replacement, credit, or refund? Required evidence and time window?
> 8. Lost-in-transit policy and tracking coverage for both routes.
> 9. Sample: price and shipping cost to [city], India.
> 10. Custom logo (debossing) — MOQ and price.
>
> Thank you,
> [name] · [brand] · [email]

## 2. AliExpress seller message (via DSers-linked listing)
> Hi, I run a small online store and may order this lick mat regularly for my customers (direct shipping, no MOQ). Could you confirm: (1) material and whether you have a food-contact test report, (2) exact dimensions and weight, (3) processing time, (4) which shipping methods give tracking to the US and Australia, (5) whether you can ship without invoices or promotional material in the parcel, (6) how you handle damaged items, and (7) price if I order 20+ per month? Thank you.

## 3. Follow-up when no reply (send after 3 business days)
> Hi [name], following up on my message from [date] about [product]. The key items for me are US-warehouse availability, test reports, and defect handling. Happy to start with a paid sample. Thanks!

## 4. Sample evaluation checklist (after sample arrives)
- [ ] Dimensions match listing (±5 mm)
- [ ] No chemical smell after wash
- [ ] Suction holds on tile and glass for 10 min with spread applied
- [ ] Grooves clean with a brush in < 60 s
- [ ] Withstands 24 h freezing without cracking
- [ ] Tear test: pinch-pull edge — no tear
- [ ] Packaging free of supplier branding/invoices
- [ ] Photos/video taken for listing (original content only)

**Rule:** a supplier's status becomes `VERIFIED` in `supplier-database.csv` only after (a) written answers to Q2–Q8, (b) test reports reviewed, and (c) a sample passes the checklist.
