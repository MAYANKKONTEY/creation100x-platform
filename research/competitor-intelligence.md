**Marketing pattern:** brands rely on educational blog posts (SodaPup), retailer presence (LickiMat), or price (private labels). Few own the "grooming-day routine" angle in short-form video — **opportunity** (hypothesis; verify with TikTok/IG audit).

**Delivery promises / refund policies:** *pending* storefront review.

**Exploitable gaps**
1. Bundles organised by *use moment* (bath, nail trim, crate, vet visit) rather than shape.
2. Honest sizing (cm + in + photo with a common object).
3. A cleaning routine and recipe library as owned content.
4. Clear, non-medical claims — differentiates from "anxiety relief" listings that overclaim.
