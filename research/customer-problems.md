# Customer Problems (evidence-based, 2026-11-18)

Quotes are paraphrased from reviews/articles cited in `sources.md`. No invented testimonials.

## Lick mats (P3, P4, P5)
| Problem | Evidence | Product/brand response |
|---|---|---|
| Mat smaller than expected | LickiMat Classic Buddy "surprised at how small" (petful) | State dimensions in cm/in with a size-reference photo next to a dog bowl |
| Hard to clean grooves | Coomazy reviews: hard to get last bits out of grooves | Include brush-friendly groove depth; publish cleaning video; dishwasher-safe only if supplier certifies |
| Mat slides / dog flips it | Suction-cup marketing implies this is the core complaint category | Suction cups + test on tile/glass; disclose they don't stick to textured/grout surfaces |
| Material safety worry | AKC: choose food-grade; non-food-grade may leach dyes | Obtain supplier test report (FDA 21 CFR 177.2600 / LFGB) before claiming "food-grade" |
| Dog chews mat | AKC: supervise; vendors warn about torn pieces | Prominent supervision + replace-when-damaged guidance |
| Doesn't fix anxiety | Enrichment vendors admit mats don't cure separation anxiety | Never claim anxiety cure; position as grooming/bath distraction and enrichment |

## Tech pouches (P8)
- Elastic loops too small for normal cords; bag bulges when full; stiff zippers; small size can't fit laptop charger.

## Gym mounts (P9)
- Magnet strength specs inconsistent across retailers; MagSafe cases alone too weak on equipment; Android needs magnetic ring.

## Cross-category purchase barriers (inferred from market structure, not measured)
- Slow international delivery and surprise customs charges (T1–T6) → biggest trust risk for a non-local store.
- Unknown brand + overseas business → needs transparent About/shipping/returns pages and a real contact address.
