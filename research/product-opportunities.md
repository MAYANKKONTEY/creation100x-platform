## Recommended initial product: Lick mat bundle
**Offer concept:** two food-grade silicone mats with different groove patterns (fast/slow), suction cups, a silicone spreading spatula, and a printed/PDF "freeze & spread" routine card. Target price **$22** (base case contribution ≈ **$7.82/order, 35.6%** before any ad spend).

### Score rationale
| Criterion | Score | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Demand 15 | 13 | 17k+ reviews on one LickiMat listing; carried by Chewy/Walmart/Target (P1–P3). Not 15 because search-trend data is still unmeasured. |
| Problem 15 | 12 | Owners use mats for bath, nail trims, vet visits, crate time (P4). Real but "nice-to-have", not a pain killer. |
| Content 15 | 14 | Dogs licking a frozen mat during a bath is a 7-second visual payoff; recipe content is endlessly repeatable and doesn't require sponsored creators. |
| Supplier 15 | 12 | Widely manufactured, simple single-part item. Docked because CJ US-warehouse stock and food-grade certificates are **unverified**. |
| Margin 20 | 13 | Price anchors are low ($4.97–$8.99 single mats). Margin exists only as a bundle; conservative case falls to 12% CM. |
| Differentiation 10 | 6 | Bundle + routine guide + calm-grooming positioning; mats themselves are commodity. |
| Risk 10 | 8 | No electronics, no batteries, no sizing. Risks: food-contact claims, chewing/ingestion → supervision warning; must avoid "cures anxiety" claims. |

### Why not the higher-headroom gym mount?
Better unit margin, but (1) magnet-strength complaints become returns, (2) phone/case compatibility questions increase support load, (3) strong magnets can trigger air-cargo restrictions, and (4) a ₹0 founder needs the lowest-defect, lowest-support first product.

### Why not the tech pouch?
Close second. Rejected as initial because the competitive set includes strong design brands (Bellroy, Peak Design, tomtoc), and differentiation would need custom manufacturing (MOQ, cash) to be meaningful.

## Key risks for the recommendation
1. **Price ceiling** — shoppers anchor to $9 LickiMat. Bundle must be visibly more (two textures + tool + guide).
2. **Supplier unverified** — no live CJ quote yet (catalogue behind bot wall).
3. **US-warehouse duty treatment** unconfirmed — if duty is charged per order, add ~$1–3 and re-run calculator.
4. **Payments** — PayPal is the only realistic gateway at ₹0; account limitation risk for new dropshippers.
5. **Claims** — no anxiety/medical claims; use "enrichment", "distraction during grooming".
6. **Low barrier to entry** — copycats are easy; brand must be built on content and routines, not the product.
