# Brand Strategy — PROPOSAL (awaiting niche approval)

## Positioning
**Calm-grooming and enrichment routines for dogs.** We don't sell "anxiety cures"; we sell easier bath days, nail trims, and crate time, backed by honest how-to content.

- **Customer persona — "Busy dog parent"**: 25–45, US/AU, apartment or suburban, owns a dog that hates baths or nail trims, watches pet content on Instagram/TikTok/YouTube Shorts, buys on Chewy/Amazon, values practical tips over cute branding.
- **Mission:** make the hard parts of dog care calmer for dogs and their people.
- **Brand promise:** practical tools, honest sizing, real instructions, no overclaims.
- **Voice:** warm, practical, plain-English; specific over hype ("freeze for 30 minutes" beats "game-changing").

## Product naming system (proposal)
`[Moment] + [Item]`: *Bath-Day Duo*, *Trim-Time Mat*, *Crate Calm Kit*. Avoid medical words.

## Visual direction (proposal)
- Palette: warm oat `#F4EDE3` background, deep teal `#1F4E4A` primary, soft clay `#D98E6A` accent, ink `#1C1C1C` text (all AA-contrast against background — verify in build).
- Type: one humanist sans (e.g., "Inter" or "Figtree") for UI; one rounded display weight for headlines. Both free (Google Fonts).
- Photography: real dogs in real bathrooms, top-down mat shots with size references; no stock "happy dog" clichés; never supplier photos.
- Packaging: unbranded polybag at launch + printed routine card (supplier insert); branded box only after product validation.

Visual guidelines and brand copy files will be written once you approve the niche and pick a name.
