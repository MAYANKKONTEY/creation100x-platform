# Questions for a Chartered Accountant / Lawyer / Bank

Bring this list, `compliance/compliance-checklist.md`, and `operations/payment-setup.md` to the consultation.

## For the CA (tax + FEMA)
1. I'm an individual in India. I'll sell on my own Shopify site to customers in the US and Australia. Goods ship from suppliers in China or US warehouses direct to customers; they never enter India. Does CGST Schedule III para 7 apply, so no GST is payable on these sales?
2. Do I need GST registration at all? Does reverse charge on imported services (Shopify subscription, apps) force registration from the first rupee?
3. Can I recover the 18% GST charged on PayPal/Shopify fees? Only if registered?
4. Is this "merchanting trade" under FEMA? Which RBI framework now applies (2026 export/import regulations)? Any time limits between receiving customer money and paying suppliers?
5. Which PayPal purpose code should I select for these receipts? Do I need an IEC?
6. How should I pay suppliers abroad (card, PayPal balance, bank remittance)? Is TCS under LRS triggered if I pay by personal card for business purchases?
7. How do I report this income (ITR form, presumptive taxation under 44AD eligibility)? What records must I keep?
8. Is a sole proprietorship + Udyam adequate, or should I form an LLP/OPC before scaling?
9. Any issue with receiving USD/AUD in PayPal and converting later?

## For a lawyer
10. Refund/returns wording that complies with Australian Consumer Law and US state rules for a foreign seller.
11. Privacy policy obligations: India DPDP Act 2023, CCPA, Australian Privacy Act.
12. Trademark: clearance search for the shortlisted brand name in India, US and Australia; cost of filing.
13. Product liability exposure for a pet product sold overseas; is insurance needed?

## For the bank / PayPal / Razorpay
14. (Razorpay) Will you enable international cards for a Shopify store whose products are fulfilled by third-party suppliers abroad?
15. (PayPal) Typical holds/reserves for new business accounts receiving export payments?
16. (Bank) Documentation needed for inward remittance from PayPal under the selected purpose code (FIRC/e-BRC)?
