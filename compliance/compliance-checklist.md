# Compliance Checklist (draft, not legal advice)

Everything here is research to prepare for a professional review. Items marked **CA/Lawyer** require confirmation from a qualified Indian Chartered Accountant or lawyer.

## India
| Area | What we found | Status |
|---|---|---|
| GST on the sale | CGST Act Schedule III para 7 (from 1 Feb 2019): goods moving from a non-taxable territory to another without entering India are neither supply of goods nor services (T7). Rulings are contested where invoicing originates in India. | **CA** — confirm it applies to Shopify sales invoiced by an Indian proprietor |
| GST registration | Thresholds ₹40 lakh goods / ₹20 lakh services; reverse charge on imported services (e.g., Shopify subscription, apps) may trigger registration regardless of turnover. | **CA** |
| GST on Shopify/PayPal fees | 18% charged on invoices; recoverability depends on registration. | **CA** |
| FEMA / RBI | Merchanting-trade rules: goods must not enter India; trader must be genuine; reporting via bank. New FEMA export/import regulations 2026 (T8). Supplier payments abroad + receipts via PayPal need correct purpose codes. | **CA / AD bank** |
| IEC | Needed for goods export purpose codes on PayPal (S4); unclear if needed for merchanting. | **CA** |
| Income tax | Business income taxable in India; maintain books (Shopify orders, PayPal statements, supplier invoices). | **CA** |
| Business registration | Sole proprietorship + Udyam is the lowest-cost structure. | **CA** to confirm suitability |

## Consumer protection & policies
- [ ] Refund policy meets destination norms (e.g., Australian Consumer Law guarantees for faulty goods — **lawyer** to confirm wording)
- [ ] Delivery times stated as ranges from supplier data, never promised
- [ ] Business identity, address and contact email visible
- [ ] No fake reviews, fake scarcity, fake timers (also FTC rules on fake reviews in the US)

## Privacy
- [ ] Privacy policy covering Shopify, PayPal, analytics, email; GDPR not triggered if EU blocked, but CCPA/Australian Privacy Act basics apply — **lawyer**
- [ ] India DPDP Act 2023 obligations as a data fiduciary — **lawyer**
- [ ] Cookie consent via Shopify's customer privacy API

## Product safety & labelling (lick mat)
- [ ] Supplier test report: FDA 21 CFR 177.2600 (rubber articles for repeated food contact) or LFGB — before using "food-grade"
- [ ] Usage warnings: supervise; remove if damaged; not a chew toy
- [ ] No medical/behavioural treatment claims ("cures anxiety")
- [ ] Country of origin shown on listing and parcel

## Restricted categories avoided
Batteries, electronics, cosmetics, supplements, food, children's toys, medical devices, weapons, IP-protected designs (pet hair roller rejected).
